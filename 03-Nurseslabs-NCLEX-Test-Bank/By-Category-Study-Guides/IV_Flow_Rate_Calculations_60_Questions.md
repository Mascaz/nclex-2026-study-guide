## Question 1 (Cloze / Fill-in)
A health care provider prescribes 1,500 mL of normal saline to be infused over seven (7) hours. The drop factor is 15 drops/mL. The nurse should set the flow rate of the IV infusion pump at how many mL per hour (mL/hour) and drops per minute (drops/minute)?

This question requires you to compute for two answers. Fill in the blanks and round your final answers to a whole number.

- Answer: BLANK 1 of 2 mL/hour or BLANK 2 of 2 drops/minute 

 Answer: Fill in the blank 1 of 2

**Correct Answer:** 

**Rationale:**
Correct answers:

• 214 mL/hour

• 54 drops/minute

Formula

 mL/h = \frac{total\ infusion\ volume(mL)}{total\ infusion\ time (h)} 

Solving for the mL per hour (mL/hour)

• Use the IV flow rate formula (mL/hour) above.

• Divide 1,500 mL (total infusion volume) with 7 hours (total infusion time) to get 214.28 mL.

• Round off your final answer to get 214 mL/hour.

 \frac{1,500\, ml}{7\, hours}= 214.3\, mL/hour 

Solving for the drops per minute (drops/minute)

• Use the IV flow rate formula above.

• Convert 7 hours to minutes by multiplying 7 hours to 60 minutes per hour and get 420 minutes.

• Divide 1,500 mL (total infusion volume) by 420 minutes (total infusion time) to get 3.57 mL/minute.

• Multiply 3.57 mL/minute to the drop factor of 15 drops/mL to get 53.55 drops per mL.

• Round up your final answer to get 54 drops per minute.

 \frac{1,500\, ml}{420\, minutes} X\, \frac{15 drops }{mL} = 53.55\, drops/minute
