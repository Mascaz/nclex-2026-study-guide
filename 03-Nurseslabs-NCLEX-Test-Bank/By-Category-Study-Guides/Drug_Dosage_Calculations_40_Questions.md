## Question 1 (Cloze / Fill-in)
A health care provider orders acetaminophen (Tylenol) supp 1 g pr q 6 hr prn temp > 101. Available: Tylenol supp. 325 mg (scored). How many supp. will you administer? Fill in the blank. Record your answer to the nearest whole number.

- Answer: BLANK 1 of 1 supp. 

 Answer: Fill in the blank 1 of 1

**Correct Answer:** 

**Rationale:**
Correct answer

 

 
• 3 supp.

 

Formula

 


Formula = \frac{Desired (D) \times Vehicle (V) }{On\ Hand (H)} = amount \ to \ administer






 

Computation


 \frac{1\, g}{325\, mg}\times \frac{1000\, mg}{1\, g}\times 1\, supp= 3.08\, or\, 3\, supp 






 

 
• 1 g is equal to 1000 mg.
 
• Convert first by dividing 1 g with 325 mg to get 0.003 g.
 
• Then, use the standard formula above.
 
• Multiply 0.003 g to 1000 mg (desired dose) to get 3.
 
• Finally, multiply 3 to 1 supp (vehicle) to get 3 supp.

## Question 4 (Cloze / Fill-in)

Computation

 \frac{15.8 \, mEq/lb}{8\, mEq/lb}\times 1\, ml= 2\, ml
