## 📂 Folder Breakdown

### 1. [`01-Official-2026-NCSBN-Materials/`](./01-Official-2026-NCSBN-Materials/)
Official blueprints and examination guides directly from the **National Council of State Boards of Nursing (NCSBN)**:
- `2026_NCLEX_RN_Blueprint_and_What_Changed.md` — Summary of the April 2026 changes (Health Equity, SDOH, Unbiased Care, NGN 6-Step CJMM, and category weights)
- `2026_NCLEX_RN_Test_Plan.pdf` — Official 2026 NCLEX-RN Test Plan PDF
- `2026_NCLEX_PN_Test_Plan.pdf` — Official 2026 NCLEX-PN Test Plan PDF
- `2026_NCLEX_Candidate_Bulletin.pdf` — Official 2026 Candidate Bulletin & Exam Rules
- `2026_NCLEX_Info_Flyer.pdf` — Official 2026 NCLEX Quick Overview Flyer

### 2. [`02-HuggingFace-NCLEX-Datasets/`](./02-HuggingFace-NCLEX-Datasets/)
Open-source nursing and NCLEX datasets exported into `.json`, `.csv`, `.xlsx`, and `.parquet`:
- `MedNurse_QA_Full.json` & `MedNurse_QA_Full.csv` — **21,647 nursing Q&A pairs** from Open RN textbooks covering Pharmacology, Fundamentals, and Clinical Skills
- `MedNurse_QA_Contrast_Set.csv` — **22,000 Q&A contrast items**
- `nursing_instruction_dataset.json` — **1,133 clinical nursing communication & scenario breakdowns**
- `nursing_pharmacology.csv` — Nursing pharmacology review dataset
- `nclex_nursing_questions.json`, `nclex_dataset.json`, `nclex_generation_data.json` — Open NCLEX practice items with rationales

### 3. [`03-Nurseslabs-NCLEX-Test-Bank/`](./03-Nurseslabs-NCLEX-Test-Bank/)
**1,707 categorized NCLEX practice questions** with full rationales for both correct and incorrect options:
- `nurseslabs_nclex_qbank.json` & `nurseslabs_nclex_qbank.csv` — Complete structured test bank
- [`By-Category-Study-Guides/`](./03-Nurseslabs-NCLEX-Test-Bank/By-Category-Study-Guides/) — **17 standalone Markdown study guides** sorted by topic (e.g., `Prioritization_Delegation_75_Questions.md`, `Nursing_Pharmacology_120_Questions.md`, `Fundamentals_of_Nursing_225_Questions.md`, `Maternity_OB_Nursing_225_Questions.md`, etc.)

### 4. [`04-High-Yield-Cheat-Sheets-and-OER/`](./04-High-Yield-Cheat-Sheets-and-OER/)
