Recommended books and resources for your NCLEX success:

**Saunders Comprehensive Review for the NCLEX-RN Examination**
The go-to NCLEX-RN content review by Linda Anne Silvestri, with more than 5,700 practice questions across the book and online, test-taking strategies, and Next Generation NCLEX items. A new edition is due in late 2026.

**Strategies for Student Success on the Next Generation NCLEX (NGN) Test Items**
The 2025 edition of Silvestri’s guide to the Next Generation NCLEX. It walks through each NGN item type with strategies for applying clinical judgment to case studies and stand-alone items.

**Kaplan NCLEX-RN Prep, Twenty-sixth Edition (2026)**
Kaplan’s 2026 edition includes a full-length practice test and Kaplan’s proven strategies for breaking down NCLEX-style questions, including the Next Generation format.

**Illustrated Study Guide for the NCLEX-RN Exam**
The 2025 edition of Zerwekh’s visual review uses cartoons, mnemonics, and memory notecards to make key nursing concepts easier to understand and remember.

**Lippincott Q&A Review for NCLEX-RN**
A question-and-answer review organized by clinical area, with rationales for both correct and incorrect answers to help you learn from every question.

**Saunders Comprehensive Review for the NCLEX-PN Examination**
Silvestri’s 2024 content review and practice questions written specifically for the NCLEX-PN exam, with test-taking strategies for practical nursing students.
